# PROJECT: COLONY
## World Bible v0.1 (Source of Truth)

> **Purpose**
>
> This document defines the canonical world, long-term narrative direction, and design philosophy of the project. Unless explicitly changed, this document is considered the source of truth for future discussions.

---

# 1. High Concept

Project Colony is a top-down shooter with RPG elements.

The gameplay consists of three interconnected loops:

1. Fighter flight through hostile insect swarms.
2. Survival arena after a crash, where the pilot repairs the fighter while defending against enemies.
3. Hub interactions including dialogue, quests, trading and story progression.

Gameplay rhythm:

- 3–5 minutes of high-intensity flight.
- 5–7 minutes of exploration, dialogue, trading and preparation.

The project is designed as a trilogy.

---

# 2. Trilogy Structure

## Prologue (Demo)

Purpose:

Introduce the world.

Introduce the gameplay loop.

Create emotional attachment to the main characters.

End with a strong mystery and a personal motivation.

---

## Game I — Discovery

Core Question:

> What really happened to the world?

Main themes:

- exploration
- archaeology
- hidden history
- trust
- survival

The player discovers:

- the true origin of insects
- the existence of Colony
- the Symbiote technology
- the original purpose of the Project

Ending:

The technology was never created as a weapon.

It was created to save humanity.

---

## Game II — Distribution

Core Question:

> Who should possess this technology?

Main themes:

- politics
- ideology
- responsibility
- power

Humanity begins rebuilding the technology.

Conflict moves from discovery to ownership.

---

## Game III — Culture

Core Question:

> Is humanity mature enough to possess this technology?

Main themes:

- civilization
- ethics
- culture
- responsibility

Core message:

Technology does not improve people.

It amplifies who they already are.

# 3. Canon World History

## Before the Catastrophe

A major space corporation launches the Colony Program.

Purpose:

Colonization of partially terraformed planets.

Terraforming requires centuries.

Human workers must survive long before planets become Earth-like.

---

## Symbiotic System

The technology consists of three organisms.

Human

↓

Symbiote

↓

Biological Armor (engineered insect)

The Symbiote is not a parasite.

It is a biological interface between the human nervous system and the living armor.

---

## Chemical Catastrophe

A global chemical disaster destroys civilization.

Research facilities are abandoned.

Prepared insects escape.

Laboratory Symbiotes survive.

Unable to reproduce through industrial processes, they begin reproducing inside insect bodies.

This was never part of the original design.

Each generation loses functionality.

---

## Colony

Some scientists survive.

They preserve knowledge.

Over generations they become the hidden organization known as Colony.

Their greatest problem:

Without industrial infrastructure they cannot manufacture complete Symbiotes.

Their civilization is slowly dying.

---

# 4. Current World

## Humanity

Lives inside isolated settlements.

Constantly fights insect swarms.

Knows almost nothing about the real history.

---

## Cult

A human faction.

Believes in Colony as a mythical higher civilization.

Possesses fragments of truth mixed with centuries of myths.

They are NOT controlled by Colony.

---

## Colony

Hidden descendants of the original scientific project.

Operate through secrecy.

Search for surviving industrial infrastructure.

Avoid direct conflict whenever possible.

---

# 5. Biology Canon

Symbiotes reproduce inside engineered insects.

Over generations they became something similar to a permanent biological organ.

Comparable to mitochondria:

Originally independent.

Now integrated into another organism.

However, reproduction without laboratory conditions is incomplete.

Modern Symbiotes are approximately 90% defective.

Most cannot complete full human integration.

Working Symbiotes are exceptionally rare.

The protagonist is simply fortunate that the particular Symbiote infecting him remains sufficiently functional.

The protagonist is NOT special.

---

# 6. Main Characters

## Protagonist

Pilot.

Ordinary human.

Accidentally receives a functional Symbiote.

Does not understand what happened.

---

## Female Lead

Navigator.

Decryptor.

Partner.

Secret Colony agent.

Mission:

Locate surviving infrastructure related to the Colony Program.

She genuinely falls in love with the protagonist.

Her mission and emotions eventually collide.

---

# 7. Demo Story

Beginning:

A settlement is running out of clean water.

An abandoned pumping station may save everyone.

Its location has been lost.

The protagonist performs transport missions.

Gradually collects fragments of old navigation data.

Repairs a military decryptor.

Reconstructs the station coordinates.

Launches the pumping station.

Returns home believing the crisis is over.

Final scene:

A highly coordinated unknown operation attacks the settlement.

The female lead disappears.

The protagonist believes she has been abducted.

He immediately launches pursuit.

Demo ends.

---

# 8. Player Knowledge

At the end of the demo the player should know:

- insect swarms behave intelligently
- someone organized the attack
- the world is more complex than expected
- the pumping station was only the beginning

The player MUST NOT know:

- the real nature of Symbiotes
- the real meaning of Colony
- the girl's true identity
- the purpose of Colony
- the original space colonization project

---

# 9. Design Principles

The player should constantly reinterpret previous knowledge.

Important concepts should possess multiple meanings.

Examples:

Colony

Player interpretation:

Insect colony.

True meaning:

Space colonization program.

Symbiote

Player interpretation:

Parasite.

True meaning:

Biological interface.

Cult

Player interpretation:

Religious fanatics.

Reality:

People preserving distorted fragments of scientific history.

The project should reward curiosity rather than exposition.

Mysteries should be answered gradually over multiple games.

---

# 10. Core Themes

- Survival versus evolution.
- Technology as an amplifier of human nature.
- Civilization versus biology.
- Responsibility before power.
- The future of humanity is determined more by culture than by technology.
